Interaction model

  • Hover intent: opening is debounced ~120ms; leaving the trigger + panel closes after ~180ms so diagonal travel toward the panel doesn't drop it.
  • Click: toggles the menu and pins it open until the user clicks a link, clicks outside, presses Esc, or opens another menu.
  • Caret rotates 180° and a teal→green underline appears on the active trigger.
  • Backdrop: a light top-gradient scrim (not a full-page dim) separates the open panel from page content.

Accessibility

  • Triggers are real <button>s with aria-expanded and aria-controls; each panel has a matching id. Nav is wrapped in <nav aria-label="Primary"> with semantic <ul> link lists.
  • Enter/Space opens or closes a menu; Esc closes and returns focus to its trigger; Tab moves naturally into and through panel links.
  • Every section's first link is an “…Overview” destination, so the top-level page is always reachable as a plain full page — menus enhance, never trap.
  • Focus rings are high-contrast and brand-aligned; ARIA is kept minimal (no menu/menuitem desktop-app semantics that would hijack normal browsing).
  • Mobile drawer sets aria-hidden, traps nothing destructively, and closes on Esc / overlay / close button.

Information architecture (CMT review build)

Seven calm primary items, each opening an intent-grouped mega menu: Personal · Loans · Business · Digital Banking · Resources · About · Support. Every approved page is accounted for — nothing was removed, only repositioned to its most intuitive home.

  • Home — single destination; the logo links to it (plus a Home chip in the mobile drawer). No duplicate “Homepage”.
  • Utility region (not pages) — Make A Payment, Rates & Fees, Locations, Contact, Routing # and Search sit in the top utility bar; Member Login, Apply For A Loan and Open An Account are the high-priority header CTAs. Visible without opening a menu.
  • Promotions — lives in Resources → Promotions & Offers (Current, Auto Refinance, Skip-A-Pay, HELOC, Seasonal, Archived), with the Resources featured card as a lighter promo entry point.
  • News & Community — lives in About → Community & Updates (overview, From The Vault, involvement, newsletter, annual meeting, scholarship/grant winners, branch updates, events, CEO updates).
  • Rates & Fees — one source of truth on its own page (utility bar), surfaced via an on-page sub-nav and cross-linked from Loans (Loan Rates) and Support (Rates & Fees, Fee Schedule). Rate/fee sub-pages are not duplicated across menus.
  • Support — promoted to a primary item: Get Help Fast, Account Services, and Forms, Fees & Policies. Routing Number links open the copy modal anywhere they appear.
  • Footer — full secondary sitemap covering all sections plus disclosures (NCUA, Equal Housing) and policy links.

Component inventory

  • UtilityBar · PrimaryHeader (Brand, PrimaryNav, CtaCluster) · MegaPanel (Eyebrow, Group×N, FeaturedCard) — all data-driven from one nav model.
  • MobileHeader · Drawer (Search, CtaGrid, Accordion×N, Footer w/ Routing+Contact+Locations) · StickyQuickActions (Login/Apply/Open/Pay).
  • Logo lockup keeps fixed clear-space; never recolor, crop, or combine with other graphics. Replace the placeholder .brand mark with the production SVG logo at the same left position.
